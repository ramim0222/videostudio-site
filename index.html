Video Studio

A private tool that uploads and schedules the videos of one YouTube channel, জানার জানালা, made and run by its owner, S. M. Ramim.

What it does

  • Runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it.
  • Uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, through the official YouTube Data API.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, thumbnail, subtitles, playlist and planned publish time, and adds the first comment once the video is public.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect or share anyone else's data.
  • It does not sell or share any data, and it shows no ads.
