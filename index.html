Video Studio Personal

A private tool that uploads and schedules the videos of one channel, জানার জানালা, made and run by its owner, S. M. Ramim.

What it does

  • Runs on the owner's own computer and the owner's own server. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it.
  • Uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, through the official YouTube Data API, and sets each video's title, description, tags, thumbnail, subtitles, playlist and planned publish time.
  • Posts them to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account at their planned time, through Meta's official API.
  • Sends them to the owner's own TikTok inbox at their planned time, through TikTok's official Content Posting API. The owner then opens each one in the TikTok app, adds the caption and presses Post there.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect or share anyone else's data.
  • It does not sell or share any data, and it shows no ads.
  • It never posts on TikTok by itself: every TikTok post is made by the owner in the TikTok app.
