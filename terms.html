Who may use it

  • Video Studio Personal is not a public service. Only its owner uses it, with the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram account and TikTok account.

What it does

  • It uploads the owner's finished videos through each platform's official API, at the time the owner planned: to YouTube (with title, description, thumbnail, subtitles and publish time), to the owner's Facebook Page and Instagram account, and to the owner's TikTok inbox.
  • On TikTok it only sends the video to the owner's inbox as a draft. It never posts on TikTok by itself: the owner opens the draft in the TikTok app, writes the caption, sets the post's options and presses Post there.
